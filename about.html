01 / ABOUT US

The ease of a hotel.
The privacy of a home.

Since 2021, Reunios has offered short-term vacation residences that hold both: resort amenities for the days you want them, and a kitchen and living room for the evenings you would rather stay in.

“Great stays don’t happen by chance. They happen when people care, when details are handled well, and when the right tools support the work behind the scenes.”

— Yamini Rathi, Founder
02 / OUR STORY

We began in 2021 with a simple conviction: a vacation should not ask you to choose between polished service and the comfort of a private house.

Our residences in Park City, Leavenworth, and Marina Dunes were composed around that idea.

Guests found us on Airbnb, where consistent hosting earned Superhost standing. That trust let the company grow with purpose: a one-million-dollar booking target in 2022, and five million by 2024. The same discipline now shapes how we hire and how we lead.
